# The relating family of named systems

We want to speak of **RMN**, **WMAP**, and **RWIL** as coherent systems — the way people say "WASM" or "ActivityPub" — even though not one of them is a dedicated module in someone's `src/` tree.
This note is the registry that makes that possible: it names the systems, says what realizes each, and points at each one's spec.

## Two planes

The confusion this resolves: "where is WMAP in the `relating/src` layout?"
It isn't there — and that is correct, because there are two different planes and they are not the same list.

- **Implementation plane — capabilities.** These ARE directories.
  In `lib/relating/src`: `typing/` (the carrier kernel), `writing/` (representation codecs), `ledger/` (the witness projection engine), `evaluating/` (the term reduction engine), `running/` (acting: dispatch/serving/transport).
  In `lib/animating`, `lib/organizing`, etc.: their own capability dirs.
  A capability is a unit of *code organization*.

- **Product plane — named systems.** These are NOT directories.
  RMN/WMAP/RWIL are *specs* realized BY one or more capabilities, possibly spanning packages.
  A named system is a unit of *identity and reasoning*.

**WASM is exactly this distinction.** No `wasm/` folder "is" WASM; WASM is a spec realized across V8, Wasmtime, and browsers, and its coherence comes from the spec and the name, not a location on disk.
ActivityPub is woven across all of Mastodon's source, yet "ActivityPub" is still a crisp, nameable system.
Our three products are the same kind of object: **a name + a spec + a set of realizing files.** That apparatus — not a directory — is what makes a system speakable.

## The three, briefly

- **RMN** is the substrate notation.
  Its moat is **Lean-certified totality**: every well-typed RMN term provably strongly-normalizes (Church–Rosser is certified in `RelMath.
  CHL`), so completing one — evaluating to normal form — sits at the decidable `scott` rung, never the Turing rung of arbitrary code.
  RMN is the first codec that legitimately carries *executable* content while staying decidable. **It is the founder of the family and currently the least formalized as a product — it has no dedicated spec note yet.** It should get one (peer to the WMAP/RWIL notes); this registry is its interim home.

- **RWIL** projects RMN *through time*.
  The log is the source; state/record/content are its channel-codes and sufficient statistics; the `log → state → record → content` chain is a Markov chain governed by the data-processing inequality (= the boundary→bulk holographic arrow).
  Proven green: an agent's WMAP outbox is *a channel-code of the RWIL log* (`outbox ≅ log`), tying RWIL and WMAP together at the seam where time is forgotten.

- **RWIL/OTLP** is RWIL projected to the observability ecosystem.
  It proves the same source/channel split at another standard wire: OTLP spans round-trip to EAVT witnesses, and the time-forgotten bulk cells agree under the data-processing inequality.
  The ledger remains the trace store; OTLP is the export channel.

- **Raft** is RWIL coordinated at the CALM frontier.
  Monotone operations keep the coordination-free path; non-monotone operations enter a leader-elected term whose total order gives the RWIL log one command per index.
  It is the coordinated dual of the CRDT join: CRDTs retain concurrent values as information, while Raft rejects the unchosen value at an index.

- **Contradiction-aware federation** composes the moat exports in one bridge.
  Protocol sources enter as RWIL readings, cross-source disagreement remains a held top instead of a winner-picked value, CALM keeps monotone disagreement coordination-free, and only the non-monotone H1 residue is submitted to Raft.

- **WMAP** projects RMN *across* the web.
  Conformance to ActivityPub is a *consequence* of the category theory (the math is the moat), capability-security is a theorem (authority by move, not identity), and disagreement is a first-class held value (assert + `Undo` → ⊤, retained).
  "WASM for skillful agents."

The shape of the family: **RMN is the substrate; RWIL projects it through time; WMAP projects it across the web; face standards such as SPARQL/RDF QUERY are owned by the faculty whose math makes the standard possible.** One notation, several derived projections.

## Recipe: naming a fourth system

A new named system does **not** require a new package or directory — proposing one because it "deserves a folder" is the decorative move to avoid.
It requires exactly four things:

1. **A name** (and its expansion), coined by the operator.
2. **A one-line pitch** a non-specialist can repeat.
3. **The realizing files** — the slice of existing capabilities that *is* it.
4. **A spec note** in `dev/docs/notes/` — the unit of coherence, the thing you point people at.
  (This is what WASM's spec page is.)

Add the row to the registry above.
If and only if a capability is genuinely missing do you add a directory — and even then the directory is a capability, not the product.
