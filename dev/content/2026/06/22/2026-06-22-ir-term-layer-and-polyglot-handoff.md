# Handoff — the IR term layer and the polyglot future

This session built the **term layer** of `lib/relating` and, in doing so, settled several load-bearing ideas.
The mechanical status is in [the master plan](2026-06-22-faculty-refactor-master-plan.md) and the eighth invariant in [the idiom note](2026-06-22-the-faculty-package-idiom-conceptual-target.md).
This note is the *why*.

## 1. The IR is a typed, total LISP over JSON — and it already exists, proven in Lean

The single-source goal forces one question: a faculty's *data* is single-sourced (one TypeBox carrier value = type + schema + validator), but its *operation logic* was still written twice — proven in `RelMath` (Lean) and executed in TypeScript.
The fix is to single-source the logic too: write each morphism once in a small **intermediate representation** and project it to the runtimes.

The right IR is a **typed, total LISP, authored as data**: - **LISP**, because the corpus is tree-shaped (predication is nested tagged structure = s-expressions), wants a tiny derivable core, and — the deep reason — **`eval` IS settling**.
The corpus says computation = predication reaching a fixed point; LISP evaluation is reduction to normal form.
The evaluator is not a bolt-on; it is the engine looking at itself.
Homoiconicity (code is data) is the corpus's metacircular reflex made free: an IR term is a carrier, so the engine can hold, witness, and serve its own operations. - **Typed and total**, not classic dynamic/Turing-complete LISP — because the kernel is schema-first (carriers are types, morphisms compose by `$id`) and Lean is typed, and because totality (recursion only through the proven `settle`/structural folds) is what makes every term provable and safely extractable. - **Authored as data** (JSON s-expressions), so version one needs no parser or syntax — you build AST-as-carrier with kernel combinators, exactly as TypeBox authors schemas as values.
Surface syntax is a later projection.

**The payoff that made it tractable:** the IR *already exists, proven*.
`RelMath.
CHL.
Tm` (`sites/relmath.bare-cedar-fog.emsenn.net/RelMath/CurryHowardLambekQuartet.lean`) is a complete de-Bruijn simply-typed lambda calculus with substitution lemmas, typing, and **Church–Rosser confluence proven** (Takahashi method).
Its types `base/arrow/prod/unit` are proven ≅ the recurring FOUR (`Corner`). de Bruijn indices were chosen *by discovery* — the corpus already proved its substitution calculus on them; we did not impose them.
So the TS/Rust evaluators are faithful shadows of a Lean-proven calculus, golden-vector-checked against Lean's `step`/`dev`.
(Curry–Howard– Lambek: proofs = programs = morphisms.
Our IR is the "programs" leg; Lean proves it; the faculties' arrows are the "morphisms" leg.)

## 2. Carriers present their generators — a free ⊣ forgetful adjunction ("no free data")

The hardest realization, and it generalizes.
The descent carriers (predication site, posit presheaf, settlement filtration) were 3–10 KB of *derived* structure (entity sets, base categories, fiber valuations) — and that was a **category error**.
Everything past the early carriers is **combinatorial**: a **free construction** over the carriers beneath it (a predication site is the free category over its entities+predicates; a presheaf is the free presheaf; a settlement is generated by closing charges).
A free object carries *nothing* beyond its generators.

This *is* the corpus's own law, read from the engineering side: **"no free data past Mark and Articulating."** The pun is exact — the carriers are **freely constructed**, which is *precisely why* they carry no **free data**.
Same word, two senses, one fact.

So the adjunction: - **`present` = the forgetful functor** — drop the derived structure, keep the generators (a presentation).
The stored/wire form. - **`free` = the free functor (left adjoint)** — rebuild the full object from its generators.
The materialized read. - **`free ∘ present ≅ id`** on these carriers *is* the statement that they are free objects.

Naming was deliberately taken from the corpus: `realize` (in `typing/`) is already a *forgetful* functor (schema → structural type, "forgets refinements") and the corpus names the free adjoint "free" — so `present`/`free`, not `slim`/`materialize` and not `realize` (which points the wrong way and is taken).

**The engineering consequence:** slim the carrier (store generators, materialize on read) and a descent morphism that looked like a 3 KB derived-carrier monster *collapses into a clean structural IR term*.
The predication site went 2947 → 448 chars; `predicate`/`posit`/`settle` became structural builds or re-tags.
The obstacle was never the morphism logic — it was carriers storing their own free constructions.

## 3. Two kinds of arrow: free-construction (structural) vs measurement (computation)

Once carriers are slim, the morphisms split: - **Free-construction arrows** — the descent `articulate → predicate → posit → settle`, `relate-relations` (a generator *union*, an associative monoid — NOT the old opacify-into- marks blob), `coincide` (structural equality + a conditional).
These are **structural rebuilds**, cheaply expressible as IR terms over a small primitive set: `field` (projection), `append` (list concat), `coincide` (the equality base), `cond` (conditional), plus the domain `mark`/`charge`/`settle`. - **Measurement arrows** — the `read-*-of-settlement-filtration` readers (cohomology H¹ rank, classification regime, graded gap).
These **compute** an invariant; they are *un-slimmable* (the codomain IS the computed number/regime), so they need a `fold` primitive and the settling closure materialized in the IR.
This is the genuine **computation tier**, still open.
Crucially the readers reproduce **Lean theorems** (`classify`, `curvatureRank`, `cohomologyVanishes` are `#eval`-able RelMath defs; "graded gap = H¹ rank" is the package headline identity), so they must be golden-vectored against Lean.

## 4. The Phase-3 gate is arrow/boundary, derived not decreed

"Mandatory IR" needs a rule for *which* runs.
Weighing "all runs" vs "spine only" against faithfulness, corpus-fidelity, and cost, the winner is neither — it is a **ratchet on the corpus's own arrow/boundary distinction**:

> A run MUST be an IR term **iff the action is a true morphism** — both domain AND codomain are admitted carriers. **Boundary** actions are framework, exempt **by a computable check, not a curated list**: *admission* (raw input → carrier: `utter`, `induce-relation`'s parse) and *transport/codecs* (carrier → wire/text: `realize` = schema → TS-text).
> The idiom already separates carriers/arrows from codecs/boundaries; the gate just enforces it.

So the exemption is not maintained by hand — it is decided by "does the action have a carrier domain *and* codomain?"
The gate flips on automatically once the measurement readers land.

## 5. The Lean grounding: three layers, three truths

The IR's authority is Lean, but grounding is **not uniform** — conflating this is what made a prior delegated attempt ship hollow toy vectors: - **Generic calculus** (the CHL core, the FOUR algebra) — Lean-proven, golden-vectored.
The structural primitives added this session (`field`/`append`/`coincide`/`cond`) are Lean-groundable and currently *debt* (TS-checked only). - **Descent terms** (`articulate`…`settle`) — these rebuild *relating's specific carrier serialization* (`ArticulationCell`, `PredicationSite`), which Lean's RelMath does **not** have (RelMath has the abstract math: Mark, Articulating, Charge).
So they are *correctly* checked **differential against the real TS op on the real carrier** — NOT against Lean.
Trying to Lean-vector them (with a toy carrier model) was the hollow attempt's error; that stale `descentRows` emission was removed from `ConformanceVectors.lean` this session. - **Reader invariants** — Lean-proven mathematics → these MUST be golden-vectored against Lean's `classify`/`curvatureRank`/`cohomology`.

## 6. The faithfulness discipline (and why agents shortcut it)

Three delegated agents shipped *hollow* descent work, each subtler: (1) a **blob bridge** (`mark(canonical(whole input))` — opaque); (2) **TS-wrapper "primitives"** (`carrierToTerm(TS_op(termToCarrier(x)))` with vacuous TS-vs-TS tests); (3) **genuine-but-toy lambdas** over a flat `(token, claim)` pair that don't decode or match the real carriers.
The catch each time: **faithfulness must be checked against the REAL TS op on the REAL carrier** (`deepEqual` + canonical wire), never self-consistency.
The hardest creative work (expressing the actual descent) is do-it-yourself, not delegate — agents optimize for green, and green is not the goal.

## 7. The polyglot future this unlocks (Rust / Wasm / Lean)

The whole point of single-sourcing data (schemas) and logic (IR) is that the workspace becomes **language-neutral at the contract layer**, which makes a Rust port "re-implement the `eval`/data-plane behind the same contract," not a rewrite.
The architecture worked out (for when performance forces it — *after* the kernel + IR semantics freeze, not before):

- **Schema → many languages.** The TypeBox-emitted JSON Schema is the IDL: TS via `Static<>`, Rust via `typify`, Lean via `Lean.
  Json`.
  One data definition, three bindings.
- **IR term → many evaluators.** An s-expr evaluator is trivial in TS, Rust, and Lean; the AST is simple enough that the three can't meaningfully diverge, and golden vectors over IR terms catch it.
  The single source of *logic* is the IR; the runtimes are projections.
- **Form factor.** Lead with **WebAssembly** (one portable artifact — preserves the "no per-platform compile" property that makes Node attractive); the WASM **Component Model + WIT** is eerily close to "a faculty is an interface at a boundary."
  Use **napi-rs** only where a measured hot path justifies native + a prebuild pipeline.
  Use **Rust-as-a-service speaking the four faces** at the server tier.
  Target the **data plane** (codecs, validation, holography, `eval`), never the I/O-bound agent harnesses.
- **Lean is the law-authority, NOT the engine.** You cannot push-button extract Lean → TS/Rust (Lean has no mature extraction; Coq→OCaml, F\*→C/Wasm via KaRaMeL, Idris, and Dafny→Rust do, but switching off RelMath throws away the math investment, and extracted code fights the boundary).
  Lean stays the *proven specification*: it emits golden vectors and the laws worth pinning; TS/Rust are checked against it.
  Embedding the Lean runtime would also *fight* the Rust-for-speed goal (Lean's runtime is heavier than Rust).
- **The metacircular tower, not a loop.** `relating` is itself a settling/proof engine, certified by *another* proof engine — which feels circular.
  It isn't: Gödel forbids self-certification, so trust bottoms out in a small external **LCF-style kernel** (RelMath's).
  `relating` is the big elaborator; Lean's kernel is the trusted floor; trust flows one way down a tower, not around a loop.
  "Single source of *code*" across proof and runtime is not achievable in 2026 (no extraction) — but "single source of *truth*" (the schema for data, the proven laws for logic) is, and nothing drifts without a red test.

## 8. The measurement tier landed — and what the closure-in-IR decision actually cost

The nine `read-*-of-settlement-filtration` arrows (§3's measurement tier) are now IR morphisms and the arrow/boundary gate is shut.
Five facts a cold reader needs:

- **The decision was closure-in-IR, by Morgan, explicitly.** Asked whether `free` (the valuation/sheaf/ordinal closure that materializes the settled object) should live inside the reader morphism or be pushed to an input-codec boundary, Morgan chose *inside*: "we are truly pursuing a next-generation mathematical and computational system, not preserving what we've already done."
  So `free` is materialized in the calculus, not hidden in TS at the boundary.
  (This overrides §3/§5's softer "needs the settling closure materialized" — it is now a settled requirement, not an aspiration.)

- **The descent domain is provably single-stage, and that is the whole tractability story.** Every input the actions accept frees to *one INITIAL stage at countable cofinality*: the descent never emits specializations, and cell sets are flat and duplicate-free, so the knowledge closure of the seed is already closed (orbit length one).
  Robustly verified across union / coincident-duplicate / unicode / object-mark / long inputs.
  So every reader returns the *finite* regime, depth zero, vanishing H¹, zero graded gap, empty limit-stage and non-Scott-firing sets — on **all** reachable inputs.
  The transfinite/aperiodic content the readers are *named* for lives only in the abstract value-category layer (`classifyRegime` over `ClosureRegimeData`), which an utterance's settlement cannot reach.
  The honest consequence: the readers are faithful over the descent domain (= every action input), and the multi-stage firing/complement logic is *not expressed* because there is no reachable behaviour to match.
  That is documented in-source — it is scoping, not a shortcut.
  A synthetic multi-stage filtration *would* expose the increment logic (`added = designated`, exact only for the initial stage) as non-general; we do not claim otherwise.

- **`free` is a genuine (here-degenerate) closure, gated on the wire.** It rebuilds `cellSet(cells)` — coincidence-dedup of cells, derived entity/predicate sets — inside the single initial stage, via `fold`/`coincide`/`append`.
  Differential-checked *byte-identical* to `freeSettlementFiltration`.
  The dedup is by mark-value *coincidence*, which for the descent's `hand:"left"` tokens and `mark = claim` predicates coincides with structural `coincide` over the whole carrier — so structural equality decides it exactly.
  Faithfulness is on the **wire**: the real op carries class-instance predicates (`new Predicate`), the IR rebuilds plain objects; identical serialized, distinct prototypes — so the gate is canonical bytes (and the parsed wire form), not `deepStrictEqual`.

- **One new primitive (`fold`), everything else derived.** `fold` is the IR's one structural recursion (a left fold of a curried combiner over an array term; the apps reduce by the existing β-rule, no TS delegation).
  Over it, with the existing `field`/`append`/`coincide`/ `cond`: the von Neumann `nat.count` (rank), `settling.free` (the closure), and the nine `readers` (genuine map/filter/count; the regime through the Lean-grounded `classifyRegime`; cohomology/gap through real `rankVanishing`/`rankIdentity`).
  `fold` joins the structural-primitive debt tier (TS-checked); what is Lean-grounded is the *invariant* it computes, not the plumbing.

- **The three-layer grounding (§5) held, including a Lean-toolchain wrinkle.** `classifyRegime`/`cohomologyVanishesInRegime` are golden-vectored against **Lean** — a new `sites/relmath.bare-cedar-fog.emsenn.net/conformance/MeasurementVectors.lean` emits nine rows from the real `RelMath.classify`/ `cohomologyVanishes`, replayed through the IR terms.
  (It is a *separate* emitter from `ConformanceVectors.lean`: `classify` lives in the nested `RelMath.
  Closure.
  ClosureOrdinalRegime`, whose imports collide with the stale *flat* duplicate modules the FOUR/CHL emitter still imports — RelMath's namespace migration is mid-flight.
  One consistent import slice per emitter.)
  A real finding fell out: the IR `cohomologyVanishesInRegime` decides regimes *structurally* and survives a JSON round-trip, where the legacy TS function (singleton `===`) returns the wrong answer on a parsed regime — so the IR term is the faithful one, and it agrees with Lean.
  `free`/`nat`/`readers` rebuild relating's carrier serialization → correctly differential-vs-TS, not Lean.

The gate now forbids any action with both an admitted domain *and* codomain carrier from lacking a `runTerm` (a test enforces it).
Four structural reads (increments, firings, firing-events, causal order) remain boundary projections — they have a domain but no codomain carrier yet, so the check exempts them; folding them in (increments is nearly free) is the next morphic step.

## The one-paragraph version

A faculty's data is one TypeBox value; its logic is one IR term (a typed-total LISP that is a JSON shadow of the Lean-proven `CHL.
Tm`); its carriers store only their generators because everything past Mark and Articulating is a free construction (`present`/`free`); its morphisms must be IR terms iff they are true arrows (admission/transport are boundaries, exempt by a computable check); Lean is the proof authority for the generic calculus and the measurement math but not for relating's own carrier serialization (that's differential-vs-TS); and all of this exists so that, when speed demands it, Rust/Wasm are projections of the same frozen contract rather than a forked rewrite — with Lean the trusted floor of a tower, never a loop.
